# The Heiress Who Wanted Real Love — Storyboard

## Video direction
Cinematic Nigerian relationship thriller with warm gold, deep emerald and near-black tones. Slow editorial camera drifts, clean cuts, selective quote cards and dry comic beats. Splendoure appears only as part of Zara’s story.

1. **The surname** — Zara introduces the problem: men become polite when they hear her name. Asset: `01-zara-portrait.png`.
2. **The investors** — Kola’s motorcycle empire and Femi’s thirty-seven-slide proposal. Asset: `03-business-proposal.png`.
3. **Undercover dating** — Zara edits herself down and Amaka calls it a low-budget spy film. Asset: `01-zara-portrait.png`.
4. **The simple plan** — a cinema-and-dinner card on Splendoure; David joins. Asset: `04-cinema-date.png` with an authored app card.
5. **The wrong cinema** — David’s cartoon mistake and terrible plot predictions unlock real laughter. Asset: `04-cinema-date.png`.
6. **An edited life** — affection grows while Zara’s fear tightens. Assets: `01-zara-portrait.png`, `02-david-portrait.png`.
7. **The lobby reveal** — her father, politicians and security expose the truth. Asset: `05-hotel-reveal.png`.
8. **One spot available** — David’s rematch plan, honest dinner and the six-prawn joke. Asset: `06-dinner-rematch.png`.
