# Storyboard — The Double Life

1. **The confession** — cast fragments, then the woman alone. “I lost the best man…”
2. **The good man** — the supplied Gold plan and the warm rooftop date with Damilola.
3. **The dangerous man** — she creates a Splendoure club plan, meets the women who join it, and encounters Tobi during their night out.
4. **Two lives** — midnight arrival, deleted messages, Dami’s gentle video call.
5. **The reveal** — elevator doors open; Dami sees everything.
6. **The line** — “I was doing too much for someone who didn’t deserve it.”
7. **Aftershock** — unanswered calls and panic attacks.
8. **Return** — back on Splendoure, searching for the self who would not destroy gentleness.

Visual grammar: slow push-ins, parallax drift, controlled film grain, hard flash cuts at the reveal, burgundy/black/gold palette. Captions stay inside the 80% title-safe region.
