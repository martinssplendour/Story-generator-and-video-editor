## Video direction

Use the blue-professional frame system remixed to Splendoure: white and softly blue-tinted canvas, deep navy display type, Splendoure blue as the primary structural accent, and the screenshot's green only for the authentic JOIN payoff. The screenshot remains one immutable image layer throughout; never rebuild, retouch, separate, or animate its internal people or UI. Motion follows smooth long-tail easing with decisive kinetic-type accents: camera movement is applied only to an outer viewport around the screenshot, while typography and highlights are separate overlays. Reveal copy sequentially across each frame rather than front-loading it. Frames 2 and 5 are deliberate held reads after their final phrase; Frame 6 holds the JOIN state through the close. Keep the bottom 17% clear except when intentionally featuring the screenshot's real JOIN control. Never add faces, people, invented UI, fake conversations, generic AI gradients, particles, glitch, excessive decoration, slideshow front-load/freeze, or independent screensaver motion.
